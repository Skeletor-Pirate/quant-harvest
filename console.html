Quant Harvest

Defer heavy post-quantum cryptographic workloads until local grid energy is clean. Atomic. Durable. Emissions-aware.

Ingestion
Post-Quantum Workload Queue
ML-KEM key exchanges, ML-DSA digital signatures, and Falcon short signatures are accepted as durable tasks. Each transaction is written atomically into a SQLite Write-Ahead Log.
02
Emissions Audit
Real-Time Carbon Grid Sensing
The scheduler polls local grid carbon intensity in real-time. When emissions exceed a configurable threshold, heavy computation locks are engaged to prevent wasteful energy consumption.
03
Threshold Guard
Adaptive Scheduling Engine
When grid carbon is high, queued cryptographic jobs are gracefully deferred. No jobs are lost — the WAL ensures full crash recovery and idempotent replay of every workload.
04
Execution
Clean Energy Commit
When emissions fall below the threshold, the gate opens. Task threads process jobs concurrently, committing results atomically. Zero data loss. Maximum efficiency.
